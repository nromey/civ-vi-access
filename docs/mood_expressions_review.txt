CIV VI ACCESS - LEADER MOOD EXPRESSIONS (for review)

These replace the flat mood label (like "Guarded") with the leader's expression,
so the diplomacy screen describes the leader's FACE instead of reading a stat.

Spoken form: <Leader> of <Civ> + the phrase.
Example: "Tokugawa of Japan watches you warily. Press Escape to continue."

The mod picks one of the five per encounter so it doesn't repeat.
This file matches the code exactly. Tell me which to change, cut, or reword -
line by line is fine (e.g. "guarded 4" or "war 5").

Note on which moods actually fire: in plain vanilla Civ VI the diplomatic states
cover ALLIED, FRIENDLY, NEUTRAL, UNFRIENDLY, HOSTILE (denounced), and WAR.
GUARDED, AFRAID, and DECEPTIVE have no direct vanilla trigger yet - they're
written and waiting, but won't be heard until we find a signal for them. They're
included below so you can review the wording now.


ALLIED  (declared friend or ally - the warmest)
1. greets you like an old friend
2. clasps your hand warmly
3. welcomes you like family
4. beams at the sight of you
5. greets you with unguarded warmth

FRIENDLY
1. smiles warmly
2. greets you with an easy smile
3. looks genuinely glad to see you
4. gives you a warm, open nod
5. brightens as you approach

NEUTRAL
1. gives you a measured look
2. regards you evenly
3. studies you without expression
4. greets you with a cool, polite nod
5. meets your eyes, unreadable

GUARDED  (no vanilla trigger yet)
1. watches you warily
2. eyes you with quiet caution
3. keeps you at arm's length
4. weighs every word before answering
5. regards you carefully, giving nothing away

UNFRIENDLY
1. frowns
2. eyes you coldly
3. greets you with a thin, joyless smile
4. looks faintly disappointed in you
5. barely bothers to hide their distaste

HOSTILE  (denounced)
1. glares at you
2. scowls openly
3. fixes you with a hard, furious stare
4. looks ready to have you thrown out
5. can barely contain their contempt

AFRAID  (no vanilla trigger yet)
1. eyes you nervously
2. shifts uneasily as you speak
3. can't quite hide their unease
4. watches you the way one watches a circling wolf
5. forces a smile that doesn't reach their eyes

DECEPTIVE  (no vanilla trigger yet - the sneaky one)
1. smiles a little too smoothly
2. grins slyly, as if at a private joke
3. offers a smile that never reaches their eyes
4. watches you with a sly, calculating warmth
5. smiles - but something underneath it doesn't

WAR
1. scowls, ready for a fight
2. regards you like a problem to be solved
3. meets you with cold, martial contempt
4. looks at you with open, weary hatred
5. squares up as if the war were already in the room
